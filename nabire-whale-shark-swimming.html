Can you swim with whale sharks in Nabire?

Yes. Nabire is a practical base for reaching whale sharks around the fishing platforms, locally known as bagans, in the wider Cenderawasih Bay area. The animals often approach the platforms because small fish gather around the nets. This creates unusually close encounters, but sightings remain natural and can never be guaranteed.

Starting point

Nabire in Central Papua, reached independently by domestic flight or ferry.

Recommended time

At least two days around Nabire, with extra buffer when possible.

Main experience

A local boat trip to swim and snorkel near whale sharks at the bagans.

Best time for Nabire whale sharks

Whale shark trips near Nabire can be possible throughout the year, but there is no single date that guarantees the best encounter. Sea conditions, fishing activity around the bagans, weather and moon phase all influence the day.

Plan for flexibility

If swimming with whale sharks is the main reason for your trip, avoid arriving with only one tightly scheduled morning. An extra buffer day gives the local team more room to respond to conditions.

Moon phase and conditions

Darker nights around the new moon may support more fishing activity and fish around the bagans. It can be useful for planning, but it is not a wildlife guarantee.

Is swimming with whale sharks safe?

Whale sharks are filter feeders and are not considered dangerous to humans. They are still enormous wild animals. Safe, respectful swimming means following the local guide and giving every animal space.

Responsible swimming

  • Never touch, chase or ride a whale shark
  • Do not block its direction of travel
  • Keep distance from the head, body and tail
  • Enter the water only when the guide says conditions are suitable

Who should reconsider?

The trip uses local boats and may involve open water, sun, waves and basic facilities. Tell us before booking if you are not a confident swimmer, have limited mobility or have relevant medical concerns.

Nabire or Kaimana for whale sharks?

Both destinations can offer exceptional marine encounters, but they suit different routes. From our own experience, Nabire is the clearer choice when swimming with whale sharks is the main priority; Kaimana is especially attractive for Triton Bay scenery and a broader marine trip.
