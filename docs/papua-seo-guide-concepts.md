# SEO and AI Search Concepts: Korowai and Asmat Guides

## Shared Strategy

The existing expedition pages are concise conversion pages. They should become comprehensive planning guides that answer informational questions first and present the private expedition naturally inside the guide.

The pages should follow the successful structure of the Timor-Leste swimming guide:

- Direct answer near the top
- Clear factual sections with descriptive H2 headings
- Practical route, cost, difficulty, safety and ethics information
- Honest limitations instead of guarantees
- First-hand experience and original images
- Detailed visible FAQ matching `FAQPage` schema
- `Article` and `TouristTrip` schema on the same page
- Strong links to the Papua overview, relevant travel story and the other expedition

No page can guarantee a top Google or AI-search position. The goal is to become the most useful, specific and easily cited result for the relevant search intent.

### Recommended Page Positioning

**Proposed title:** `Visit the Korowai in Papua: Expedition Guide 2026`

**Proposed H1:** `Korowai Expedition in Papua: The Complete Planning Guide`

**Core promise:** A realistic guide to reaching Korowai areas, route options, physical difficulty, costs, treehouse expectations, ethics and private expedition planning.

### Recommended Sections

1. **The short answer**
   Explain that visiting is possible, difficult and dependent on local permission, route conditions and realistic expectations.

2. **Who are the Korowai?**
   Concise cultural and geographic context without sensationalism.

3. **Can travelers visit the Korowai?**
   Directly answer the main query and explain what a respectful visit can and cannot promise.

4. **How do you get to the Korowai?**
   Compare route logic via Agats, water and overland segments, and why charter-plane marketing is often unrealistic.

5. **Korowai treehouses: expectation versus reality**
   Address the highest-interest topic while clearly explaining staged treehouse tourism.

6. **What a private Korowai expedition can look like**
   A flexible day-by-day outline with buffers and alternative decisions.

7. **How difficult is a Korowai expedition?**
   Fitness, heat, humidity, trekking, boats, sleeping conditions and waiting time.

8. **How much does a Korowai expedition cost?**
   Explain the cost drivers and why responsible quotes are individual.

9. **Best time to travel**
   Weather and route-condition explanation without inventing a guaranteed season.

10. **Safety, permits and changing plans**
    Explain current checks, local decisions, medical preparation and travel insurance.

11. **Ethical Korowai travel**
    Permission, photography, payment, performances and treating people as hosts rather than attractions.

12. **Korowai versus Asmat**
    A simple comparison table that helps travelers self-select.

13. **Why Twin Expeditions**
    First-hand experience, local coordination, private groups and honest refusal when a plan is not appropriate.

## AI Search Requirements

Each important section should begin with a short answer that can stand alone when quoted by a search assistant. Use plain factual language, then add nuance below it.

Recommended technical additions:

- Add `Article` JSON-LD with headline, description, image, author, publisher and `mainEntityOfPage`
- Expand `TouristTrip` JSON-LD with itinerary, location, tourist type and offer details
- Keep visible FAQ and `FAQPage` JSON-LD text aligned
- Add descriptive image alt text and captions based on what is actually visible
- Add a comparison table with explicit Asmat/Korowai differences
- Show a visible updated date
- Link first-hand travel stories as supporting experience
- Avoid unsupported superlatives, guarantees and sensational claims

## Recommended Build Order

1. Expand the English Korowai guide.
2. Expand the English Asmat guide.
3. Review facts, routes and wording with local operational knowledge.
4. Build equivalent German guides based on German search intent rather than literal translation.
5. Update sitemap dates and strengthen internal links from the Papua overview and travel stories.
